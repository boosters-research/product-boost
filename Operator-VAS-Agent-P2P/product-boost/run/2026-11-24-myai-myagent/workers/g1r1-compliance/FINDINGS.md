## Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita section 319

Opened on the India Code Act PDF and on the Gazette of India Extraordinary text of Act No. 45 of 2023, 25 December 2023. Both print the same section.

Section 319 prohibits cheating by personation. Subsection (1) says a person cheats by personation if he cheats by pretending to be some other person, or by knowingly substituting one person for or another, or representing that he or any other person is a person other than he or such other person really is. The Explanation says the offence is committed whether the individual personated is a real or imaginary person. The illustrations are a person who cheats by pretending to be a certain rich banker of the same name, and a person who cheats by pretending to be B, a person who is deceased. Subsection (2) punishes whoever cheats by personation with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to five years, or with fine, or with both.

The opened section does not contain the words voice, clone, call, subscriber, or telephone. A later amending Act of section 319 was not opened. Commentary pages that discuss voice cloning under section 319 were not used as the statute.

## Voice authentication after March 2024

BSP Circular No. 1213, Series of 2025, was opened on the Bangko Sentral published-issuance PDF and on the BSP issuances PDF the circular names. Monetary Board Resolution No. 521, dated 22 May 2025, approved it. The signature block on the published PDF is dated 30 May 2025. The published page is dated 10 June 2025.

The circular says covered BSFIs must adopt strong authentication mechanisms to ensure the integrity of customer-initiated transactions, and that these include any of a listed set. Item aa is biometric authentication. Its examples are fingerprint scanning, facial recognition, and voice recognition, among others. Covered institutions are those engaged in complex electronic products and services and handling high aggregate values of online transactions. The circular defines that value as an average monthly network value of at least 75,000,000 Philippine pesos for the last six months. Section 7 says the circular takes effect fifteen calendar days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation. Section 6 says BSFIs shall comply within one year from the effective date.
